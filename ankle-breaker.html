HOW TO SURVIVE

Sharp turns: A/D or ←/→ (touch: drag on the left). You turn on a dime, but hard cuts cost speed.
STOP: S / ↓ stops you dead, instantly. Let go to run again.
JUKE: Space: fake the way you're moving, cut the other way.
Camera: V (3rd / 1st person)
They are always a little faster than you, but they react a beat late. Let one get close, then juke, stop dead, or cut left-right-fast right before it grabs you: it bites on the move and its ankles snap. Closer = more points. But they get smarter: later in the run they'll read you, stop, and keep hunting. Red runners lunge. Dodge the lunge. Check your rear-view mirror and radar. P pauses, M mutes.
